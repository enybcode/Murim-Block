Murim map — where each structure sits

Fixed map: the same place in every world. Positions checked on the real generator (block coordinates, biome, ground height over the footprint, water). North is up.
Rules followed. 1. The player starts in the centre and goes outward: safe heartland (plains, waterlands), danger at the edges (north wastes, west peaks, south jungle). 2. Each sect sits where it stands in the genre's real China, mapped onto our zones. 3. Orthodox sects in the heartland and the mountain rims; unorthodox and demonic ones in the far corners. 4. Each great house sits in the zone its seat names. 5. The Northern Blade factions keep their reserved sites. 6. No two big sites closer than ~400 blocks, none on a river or lake except the ferry, the Beggars’ hall and the port.
