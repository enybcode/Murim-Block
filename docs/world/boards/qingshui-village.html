Qingshui village 清水村 · "clear water"

Mockup. The world spawn, (564, -389) on the central plains, ground Y 67-69. 128 x 128 blocks, north up; the yellow river ferry inn is ~230 blocks west.
The feel. A quiet Song-dynasty farming village where a mortal's story starts: grey tile roofs, red pillars, a clear brook and its willow pond, millet fields all around. Small and readable: from the square you see every place you need on day one. The road from the ferry comes in under a red archway, crosses the square and leaves east towards Shaolin.
